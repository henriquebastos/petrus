# CV20.DS3 — Live understanding

## Intent

Give a person one coherent way to arrange and understand a Net before motion,
inspect observed or simulated evolution, and explore a protected hypothetical
branch. Petrus owns portable semantics and provenance; Arx owns interactive
presentation and file custody. Arx never enters Engine execution or effect
authority; it asks Petrus to compute semantic successors.

## Scope

- Deliver a strict Petrus-owned portable document with one exact canonical Net
  definition, optional portable view, and optional forkable execution lineage.
- Preserve ES-061's dense sequential integer ids, explicit smaller parents,
  and explicit `observed`/`simulated`/`manual` provenance. Every entry has the
  same direct complete-marking shape; History is optional debug metadata.
- Keep view outside canonical Net identity and execution lineage.
- Define strict parse, validation, projection, serialization, conformance
  fixtures, observed materialization, and simulated materialization.
- Hand the accepted contract to Arx for shared-canvas zoom, pan, arrangement,
  timeline navigation, inspection, “Explore from here,” Save As, and reopen.
- Advance a selected marking through Petrus rather than through Arx-local
  semantic recomputation. The application-bound path uses application bindings
  and pauses for hypothetical external results instead of performing effects.
- Support a request to fire one chosen transition or continue until a chosen
  place, transition, effect boundary, completion condition, or safety stop.
  Petrus may advance through pure transitions, but when several transitions
  could continue the requested path, it pauses for a person's choice instead
  of selecting silently or expanding every branch.
- Treat action, elapsed-time, retained-state, and output budgets as termination
  and resource safeguards. Do not reject a trusted application-owned Net merely
  because it exceeds fixed place, transition, or arc counts.
- Join canonical semantic state with correlated best-effort operational progress
  only after DS1 supplies a concrete live consumer; listeners never block motion.

## Delivery sequence

- **Done:**
  [CV20.DS3.TS1 — Portable definition and view document foundation](cv20-ds3-live-understanding/cv20-ds3-ts1-portable-definition-view/index.md)
  owns strict envelope, definition identity, node-position view, projection,
  owner fixtures, and the contract Arx consumed for the static V5 route.
- **Done:**
  [CV20.DS3.TS2 — Forkable execution lineage foundation](cv20-ds3-live-understanding/cv20-ds3-ts2-forkable-lineage/index.md)
  owns direct-marking lineage validation, producer materialization, and
  conformance fixtures from ES-061's simplified accepted model.
- **Done in Arx CV9.DS26:** arrange the real 108-place, 137-transition,
  570-arc Hamsterdan V5 Net on the shared canvas; zoom, pan, move, save, close,
  and reopen without changing definition identity.
- **Done in Arx CV9.DS27:** navigate one uniform lineage, protect observed
  entries, create a manual child through Save As, and append a Petrus-hosted
  simulated lineage below the selected marking.
- **Planned follow-up — application-bound interactive V5 simulation:** use
  Hamsterdan-owned bindings to compute one selected transition or a requested
  path segment. Advance through unambiguous pure transitions; pause for human
  choice when the path branches and for hypothetical results at external-effect
  boundaries. Append every Petrus-computed successor to the same lineage. Do
  not relabel Arx-local token manipulation as Petrus execution, and do not carry
  the current `implementation-free-v1` profile forward as a separate Arx mode.

## Acceptance / Done condition

1. Definition-only, arranged, observed, simulated, manual, and mixed-fork
   documents use one strict envelope and one navigation entry model.
2. Definition identity, direct complete markings, ids, parents, head,
   provenance, strict metadata, and malformed inputs are mechanically verified.
3. Arx opens the exact Hamsterdan V5 definition on its shared canvas and its
   node arrangement survives save/close/reopen with unchanged definition bytes.
4. An observed document remains protected; exploration creates a distinct
   document and cannot be presented or exported as original observed truth.
5. One consumer loop navigates observed, manual, and simulated entries by
   selecting their marking directly, without replaying History.
6. Live semantic and operational presentation preserves provenance and cannot
   delay, authorize, or mutate Engine motion.

## Driver QA and evidence plan

- **Complete:** strict positive/negative document round trips, owner fixtures,
  observed and simulated materialization, V5 arrangement/reopen, manual fork,
  hosted simulation append, and full Petrus/Arx gates.
- **Remaining:** execute the application-bound V5 transition path and the live
  semantic/operational join without granting Arx effect or History authority.
  Acceptance must cover one-transition execution, an unambiguous pure path,
  human choice at an ambiguous branch, a hypothetical Activity result, an
  unreachable-target safety stop, and a Hamsterdan-scale Net without topology
  count refusal.

## Out of scope

- Changing one Instance's canonical linear History into a branch graph.
- Making every inspectable point resumable.
- Giving Arx credentials, provider clients, History writer authority, or host
  lifecycle ownership.
- Performing real external effects during a hypothetical simulation.
- Optimizing repeated complete markings without measured file-size evidence.
